# Product marketing context

Updated October 2, 2026. Selected runtime: v0.1.1. Audience and commercial assumptions are hypotheses until the pilot produces evidence.

## Product overview

**One-liner:** Who did they follow? See changes in a public Instagram following list, with dated observations your AI agent can explain.

OrbitDiff is a local Python CLI plus an Agent Skill. The runtime collects and compares; the skill instructs a compatible agent. This release is a public-following preview. Its first useful action is an offline demo with synthetic accounts.

**Category:** public following history for local agent workflows.
**Business model:** MIT-licensed software. No paid offer, customer count or validated willingness to pay is established here.

## Audience and job

Founder hypothesis: adults curious about a crush or partner's public following changes. The reachable first cohort also has a compatible coding agent, terminal access and Python 3.11+.

The job is to identify a newly appearing public handle and inspect what the observations establish. A secondary job is to keep a dated, inspectable history. Reduced manual checking and wider consumer demand are unvalidated.

This route does not fit someone expecting a turnkey consumer account, private-profile access, exact action times or automatic daily alerts in v0.1.1.

## Alternatives and switching

Manual list checking is familiar but does not itself produce this tool's dated event history. Notes and screenshots require interpretation. Snoopreport is a relevant commercial alternative; the dated [study](../docs/marketing/snoopreport-study.md) describes its messaging, not a verified capability comparison. Choosing not to monitor is a valid alternative.

**Push hypothesis:** difficulty remembering what changed.
**Pull hypothesis:** a clear handle, observation dates and inspectable local evidence.
**Habit:** opening the list manually.
**Anxiety:** setup effort, session handling, incomplete observations and agent-host data handling.

No interviews or sales objections were collected. Likely objections and factual responses:

| Question | Response |
|---|---|
| Will it run every day and notify me? | This release has local scheduler guidance, no built-in alerts. |
| Can it tell me when the Follow action happened? | It records observation times. |
| Do I give the agent my password? | The offline demo needs no login; live use requires a human-created local session. |
| Does local-first mean everything stays off the internet? | OrbitDiff stores locally; the chosen agent host handles its context under its own settings. |

## Message and voice

Lead with the question, then the public-list benefit, one synthetic example, and one demo action. Use curious, direct, discreet, grounded, lightly playful language. Results and failures are literal. Discreet describes tone, not anonymity or undetectability.

**Primary CTA:** Try the offline demo with your agent.
**Founder language:** "Who did they follow?" and curiosity about a crush or partner.
**Verified customer quotations:** none. Do not convert founder language into testimonials.

Use: public following list, first observed, pending, confirmed, observation dates, incomplete check.
Avoid promises of exact action times, relationship conclusions, universal agent compatibility or automatic alerts for this release. Report observed facts and keep identity, gender, motives, attraction and fidelity unknown.

## Goals and decisions

First goal: people understand the public scope, changed handle and observation dates, then complete the offline demo with visible prerequisites. Second goal: learn which setup steps require help.

Keep M1 curiosity as the default until human evidence supports a different message. Use [measurement](../docs/marketing/measurement.md) and [pilot](../docs/marketing/pilot.md) for consented manual research. Stars and views are supporting signals, not proof of useful interpretation.

Publication, participant contact, final feedback-dependent creative, pricing and broader launch remain separate decisions. No telemetry or new recurring process is part of this marketing work.
