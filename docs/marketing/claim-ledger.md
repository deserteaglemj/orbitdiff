# Current marketing claims

Updated October 3, 2026. Selectively adapted from the marketing candidate; older v0.1.1 claims are not the current product contract. Published preview v0.2.2 remains distinct from unreleased candidate 0.2.3.

| Reader question | Supported wording | Evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who did they follow? | See public handles added after your baseline, with first-observed and confirmed dates. | Authorized live collection and later comparison. |
| What confirms a change? | Another complete observation agrees on that relationship change. The entire rosters need not match. | Live events remain separate from fixture proof. |
| Is the list complete? | Complete pagination and exact reported-count/unique-ID agreement are required. | Agreement is observational evidence, not exhaustive action knowledge. |
| Exactly when did it happen? | Dates show when the change was observed and confirmed. | Exact Follow action time is unsupported. |
| What happens on failure? | Retain existing evidence and show a gap or blocking issue. | No-change conclusions require complete observations. |
| Can it check daily? | Candidate 0.2.3 has opt-in Codex daily jobs; published v0.2.2 lacks these commands. | Manual live proof, authorized registration, actual invocation and three actual daily windows. |
| Will I receive alerts? | Candidate 0.2.3 stores confirmed additions in durable digests for macOS current-user submission. | Submission, device display, human read and real-event delivery each need separate proof. |
| Is setup automatic? | Ordinary installation stays inactive. Start with the isolated offline demo. | Human setup/comprehension and fresh agent-host sessions. |
| What about my own account? | Owner exports show snapshot observations with supplied dates and coverage. | Export observations are not live-confirmed public-following events. |
| What does a follow mean? | The account appeared in an observed public list. | Identity, gender, motives, attraction and relationship judgments remain unknown. |
| Is it better or faster? | Local storage, dated evidence and explicit confirmation are product facts. | Comparative superiority, savings, demand and pricing are unvalidated. |
