# Consented comprehension and setup study

Prepared for the current preview routes. Comprehension: **0/5**. Setup: **0/5-10**. Results remain Unproven; agents do not count as participants.

## Recruitment drafts

Comprehension: "Would you try a 15-minute adult-only research session about OrbitDiff, a tool for public Instagram following observations? You'll view a fictional example and explain what it means. No Instagram login, real targets or account data. Participation is optional; you can stop or skip any question. This is research, not a product endorsement."

Setup: "Would you try a 30-45 minute adult-only offline setup study? You'll choose a compatible preview route and try its synthetic demo. We'll record prerequisite time, help and failures, including missing tools. No Instagram login or collection. You can stop at any point. Please don't share credentials or private account data."

Owner: User supplies consenting unfamiliar adults and compatible setup participants, then approves exact invitations and recipients. These drafts have not been sent. Record overlap between groups.

## Consent and private records

"We are testing the explanation and setup, not you. We use fictional accounts only. May we keep brief notes about your answers and setup difficulties? You may decline, skip questions or stop. We use an anonymous participant ID. Recording and publishing a quotation each require separate permission."

Record adult self-attestation, notes consent, recording consent and quote permission separately. Agree contact/deletion procedure and retention date before recording. Proposed raw-note retention is 30 days after analysis, subject to the participant's agreement and authorized deletion. Collect no relationship status, Instagram account or credentials. Keep raw records outside Git with private permissions; publish only approved anonymous aggregates.

## Facilitation

1. Freeze the [versioned cards](assets/message-cards.md), file hash and copy of the selected setup instructions. Assign two first exposures to M1, two to M2 and one to M3, shuffled privately.
2. Show one whole card. Record the first unaided answer before teaching. Ask the tasks below.
3. Show the labeled current synthetic screenshots. Record all assistance separately.
4. After primary scoring, show alternatives in counterbalanced order. Later preferences are exploratory, not conversion evidence.
5. Debrief the evidence rules and ask what confused them or felt unhelpful. Preserve voluntary stopping as useful feedback.

## Participant tasks

Explain what OrbitDiff is for, which handle changed, what the two dates mean, what the first check does, what pending means, and what an incomplete check permits you to conclude. Find the offline setup action. Explain whether this selected release checks daily after ordinary installation. Stop where you would naturally stop.

| Pending | Another complete observation must agree on the relationship change. |
| Gap | Existing evidence is retained; no new conclusion follows. |
| Setup | Use the selected route's actual prerequisites; synthetic demo needs no login. |
| Readiness | Published v0.2.2 lacks daily commands; candidate 0.2.3 is opt-in with live gates unproven. Installation stays inactive. |

Diagnostic target: at least **4/5 independently understand each of scope, handle, dates, baseline and pending**. Report every criterion as n/5, plus missing assessments and helped scores separately. Any exact-time, private-access or automatic-readiness error triggers copy review even if the composite passes. Fix the confusing passage and retest a new frozen version. Five people do not prove conversion lift; retain M1 if inconclusive.

## Setup observation

Five to ten consenting compatible users try the [offline prompt](../prompt.md) or selected app preview with synthetic data. Record source/runtime/host/OS, prerequisite time separately from install/demo time, autonomous completion, helped completion, failures, stop reasons and preservation. Missing tools stay in the denominator. Participant authority governs installation and host configuration.

The private worksheet fields are anonymous ID, consent/retention choices, stimulus hash, exposure order, unaided responses, criterion scores, help, prerequisites, phase times, result, redacted error and stop reason. Screen-reader testing with an actual assistive-technology user is a separate accessibility gate. An automated axe pass is not that evidence.
