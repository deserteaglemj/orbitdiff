# Voice and messaging

**Who did they follow?**

See which accounts appeared in someone's public Instagram following list, with dated observations your AI agent can explain.

Lead with the reader's curiosity. Show one synthetic handle, one change and two observation dates. Then offer **Try the offline demo with your agent** and link the [setup prompt](../prompt.md). Keep the applicable prerequisites and preview limits beside the action.

Use curious, direct and calm language. A crush or partner is a recognizable situation, not evidence about why anyone followed an account. Light humor belongs in discovery: "The list changed. The plot is still unknown." Results and errors stay factual.

| Moment | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | pixel_forge was first observed October 1. Awaiting another agreeing complete observation. |
| Confirmed | pixel_forge was first observed October 1 and confirmed October 2. These are observation dates. |
| Gap | Today's check was incomplete. Existing evidence is retained; no new conclusion is available. |
| Daily setup | Choose a supported candidate and verify a manual workflow before enabling daily alerts. |

M1 curiosity remains the provisional default. M2 history and M3 agent workflow change only the hook in the [message cards](assets/message-cards.md). No winning variant, conversion lift or demand has been measured. The [pilot](pilot.md) determines whether people understand the result and can use the setup.

The [marketing context](../../.agents/product-marketing-context.md) owns positioning; the [claim ledger](claim-ledger.md) owns supported claims. Confidence comes from visible evidence and understandable limits, not accusations, urgency or invented social proof.
